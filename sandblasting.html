Common questions

Can I contact you when I need sandblasters for a project?

Yes. Tell us the location, type of work, duration and number of people required. ChromoBoz reviews the scope and personnel availability before confirming a collaboration.

What information is needed for an abrasive blasting enquiry?

Send the location, surface photographs, dimensions or estimated surface area, condition of the metal, preparation requirements and schedule.
